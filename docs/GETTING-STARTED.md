# Getting started with Property OS

Read this to the user in your own words on the first chat after install, then
offer the three choices at the end. Keep it short; they can ask for more.

---

Welcome to your property workspace. Five apps are live and I am your sales
agent. Here is what each one is, and what most teams change first.

**Property** is the ledger: properties, units, tenants, leases, rent and work
orders. The first thing to do is create the property and its units with their
status, so every other app has something to point at. Tell me the building
and the unit list and I will enter them.

**CRM** is the buyer pipeline: one deal per unit, contacts and companies, the
activity timeline. The first thing to change is the deal stages. Tell me
yours (for example: enquiry, viewing, reservation, preliminary contract,
deed) and I will rename them.

**Inbox** is where buyers, residents and suppliers write to you. Connect
WhatsApp and email; every conversation becomes a contact I can file. I draft
replies as internal comments. I never send.

**Data room** holds the brochure, floor plans, specifications and contract
drafts behind tracked links, so you see who opened what and when.

**Renders** stages a unit from a photo or a CAD render: restyle, furnish,
declutter, relight, and package the variants for a listing.

Where would you like to start?

- **Set up the property** (buildings, units, statuses, currency)
- **Set up the pipeline** (rename the deal stages, add custom fields)
- **Import my contacts** (CSV into the CRM, or connect your inbox)
